Acme Corp Employee Handbook (2026)

1. Leave Policy:
Employees are entitled to 24 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per year. Sick leave is separate and allows up to 10 days per year. Unused PTO up to 5 days can be carried over to the next calendar year.

2. Remote Work Policy:
Acme Corp operates on a hybrid model. Employees must work from the Kochi or Bengaluru office on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays. Mondays and Fridays are remote work days.

3. Expense Reimbursement:
Travel and client meal expenses must be submitted via the internal portal within 15 days. Internet reimbursement is capped at ₹1,500 per month.
